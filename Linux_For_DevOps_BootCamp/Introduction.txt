There are 4 layers:

Hardware    - keyboard, mouse, etc..
kernel      - core of the OS, talks to hardware, cpu management 
Shell/CLI   - used to inetaract with OS, bash, zsh
Application - node.js, browsers, we talk to os, and then os interacts with the hardware 

linux is a kernel,
ubuntu, debian, centos -- distributions of linux
 
Fast, secure, free and extremely scalable


Terminal vs Shell vs Kernel

Terminal is just a display.
Shell talks to Kernel and talks to os
Kernel executes our commands through hardware
